# RULE BOOK

## Pieces Included in the Game (Physical Version):
- Gameboard
- 1 Dice
- Player pieces
- Game timer
- 6 whiteboards & 6 dry erase markers
  - 1 whiteboard for keeping track of points
  - Other whiteboards are used for drawing

## Minimum player count: 3

Player count can scale up to 6 or more


## *How to play* —>

Players move around the board and draw on a round-by-round basis, earning points until the game ends.

### Begin round

1. A ***judge*** player is chosen.

### Players move

2. Each player, except the judge, rolls a dice for a number. The number tells them how many spaces to move on the board.

**The judge** will not move on the board.

Players will land on spaces that give ***modifiers***.
- Modifiers give players rules to follow while drawing.
- There are ***global*** and ***local*** modifiers. Some spaces do not have modifiers.

### Modifiers

***Global*** modifiers are modifiers players can land on that apply to everyone in the round. Every player in the round has to follow its rule. 

***Local*** modifiers are modifiers the player can land on that apply only to the player who lands on it. Only this player has to follow its rule.

Modifiers only apply to the **current round**.

### Paths

Some spaces on the board ask the player to move to the inner path or the outer path, as indicated by <——>
- The outer path has relaxed modifiers.
- The inner path has aggressive modifiers.

### Players *draw*

3. After all players are finished moving, the judge picks a topic for the players to draw.

**The judge** does not draw.

Drawing is ***timed***.
The default time is 60 seconds.
Global modifiers may change the time per round.

The **judge is responsible** for keeping track of time, and each players points for that round. 

4. Once players are done drawing for the round, the **judge picks a winner** based on their personal feelings. They might choose:
- The funniest drawing
- The best drawing
- The most unexpected drawing
- Anything else

The **winning player earns a point**, and the point is tallied. The tallied whiteboard is given to the next judge. 

### End round. Begin next round

## Notes 

- Both global and local modifiers from the previous round are **forgotten**. **New modifiers will be chosen per round** as the players land on new spaces on the board. 

- **Each round, a new judge player is chosen** by rotating clockwise.

## How to win 

- The winner of the game is decided by how many points they’ve earned by the time the game ends
- The game ends when the players decide

## *Space modifiers* —>
What does each space modifier mean? 

### Local Modifiers

**Draw with Triangle**: Draw using only triangular shapes.

**Draw with Circle**: Draw using only circular shapes. 

**Draw with Square**: Draw using only square shapes

**Draw with Pentagon**: Draw using only pentagon shapes.

**Two Fingers**: Draw using only two fingers (It does not matter how you hold the marker, just draw while holding the marker with two fingers. In the online version, your marker is wobbly.)

**Switch hand**: Draw only with your non-dominate hand.

**Don’t Lift Hand**: Draw without lifting your marker off the whiteboard. 

**Don’t Erase**: Do not erase while drawing.

**Switch Places**: Players who land on this space must choose another player to switch places with on the board. 

**Switch Boards**: Halfway through a drawing round, whoever lands on this space, must choose someone next to them to switch whiteboards with. After that, continue to draw until the time is up.

**Eyes Closed**: Draw with your eyes closed. 

**Sabotage**: Choose another player’s work to erase or draw over during the drawing round. You can choose anytime to do this during the drawing round, but only get one chance.

**Upside Down**: Draw an upside down image.

**X-Spaces**: You can not draw for the current round.

**←→ spaces**:  If you are on the outer board space, move to the inner board space. If you are on the inner board space, move to the outer board space.

### Global Modifiers

**Rotate Clockwise**: When a player lands on this space, In the middle of the drawing round, every player must hand over their whiteboards clockwise. After that, players must finish each other's drawings.

**40s**: If a player lands on a space with this, Everyone must draw within 40 seconds in the current round.

**20s**: If a player lands on a space with this, Everyone must draw within 20 seconds in the current round.

**10s**: If a player lands on a space with this, Everyone must draw within 10 seconds in the current round.

**+30s**: If a player lands on a space with this, Everyone gains an additional 30 seconds of drawing time in the current round.
