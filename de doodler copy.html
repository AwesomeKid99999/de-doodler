DE DOODLER — Official Rulebook

🎲 How to Play

  1. Choose a Judge: Rotates clockwise every round. The Judge does not move or draw.
  2. Roll & Move: Every other player rolls the 6-sided die and moves clockwise around their current path.
  3. Paths & Portals (<-->): Outer Path has relaxed modifiers; Inner Path has aggressive modifiers. Landing on <--> warps you between Outer and Inner paths!
  4. Pick Topic & Draw: Judge chooses a topic. Everyone draws for 60s (unless changed by a Global Time Modifier).
  5. Judging: Judge picks the funniest, best, or most unexpected drawing to earn 1 Point on the tally whiteboard!
  6. Winning: Previous round modifiers reset each round. Game ends whenever players decide — highest points wins!

🌍 Global Modifiers (Affect Everyone)

  • Rotate Clockwise: Halfway through the drawing round (50% time), every player passes their whiteboard clockwise and must finish someone else's drawing!
  • 40s: Everyone has only 40 seconds to draw this round.
  • 20s: Everyone has only 20 seconds to draw this round.
  • 10s: Lightning round! Everyone has only 10 seconds to draw.
  • +30s: Bonus time! Everyone gains +30 seconds (90s total) to draw this round.

🎯 Local Modifiers (Apply Only to the Player Who Lands on Them)

△ Draw with Triangle: Draw using only triangular shapes.
○ Draw with Circle: Draw using only circular shapes.
□ Draw with Square: Draw using only square shapes.
⬠ Draw with Pentagon: Draw using only pentagon shapes.
✌️ Two Fingers: Hold marker with two fingers (adds dual-finger wobble jitter!).
🤚 Switch Hand: Draw using your non-dominant hand (e.g., left hand instead of right)!
☝️ Don't Lift Hand: Draw in one continuous stroke without lifting your marker!
🚫 Don't Erase: Eraser and Clear tools are disabled for this round.
🔀 Switch Places: Immediately choose another player to swap board positions with.
🔄 Switch Boards: Halfway through the round, whoever landed on this space chooses someone next to them (left or right) to switch whiteboards with!
🙈 Eyes Closed: Draw blindfolded — strokes are hidden until time expires!
💣 Sabotage: One-time power during the round to scribble over or erase part of another player's drawing!
🙃 Upside Down: Draw your picture upside down! Flipped 180° for the Judge.
❌ X-Spaces: You sit out and cannot draw for the current round.
<--> Portal Spaces: Warp directly between the Outer Path and Inner Path!
